50cc vs 125cc Scooter Rental in Barcelona

If you’re planning to rent a scooter to explore Barcelona, you might wonder whether a 50cc or 125cc model is the right choice. This guide explains the key differences so you can pick the best option for your trip.

50cc vs 125cc Scooter Rental in Barcelona

Local guide to choosing between 50cc and 125cc scooter rental in Barcelona, including licence requirements, best routes and practical tips from RSB.

Licence requirements

For 50cc scooters, Spain generally accepts an AM licence or a valid B car licence. For 125cc scooters, Spain generally requires an A1 licence or a valid B car licence held for more than 3 years, within Spanish territory. Foreign licences and international permits must be checked case by case.

Performance and comfort

50cc scooters are light and practical for Barcelona traffic and narrow streets. 125cc scooters offer more power and comfort, especially for hills, longer journeys or riding with a passenger.

Best routes for 50cc

With a 50cc scooter you can explore the Gothic Quarter, Barceloneta and Montjuïc. The speed is enough for urban traffic and parking is usually easy.

Best routes for 125cc

A 125cc scooter is better for longer routes, hills and riding two-up. It is a better choice if you plan to go beyond the central beach and city areas.

Prices and what is included

At RSB, 50cc and 125cc scooters use the same reference price table shown on the Prices page: full day €35, 24 hours €40, 2 days €80, 3 days €115 and extra day €30. Helmet, third-party insurance and basic assistance are included.

Where to pick up your scooter

Our shop is in Vila Olímpica, next to Port Olímpic. Book by WhatsApp and collect your scooter close to the beach. For scooter rentals of 3 days or more, hotel delivery can be arranged.

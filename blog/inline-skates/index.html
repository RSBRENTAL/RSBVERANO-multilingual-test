What to know before renting inline skates

A practical overview before you choose a session

RSB Rental Scooter Barcelona has a privileged location in Vila Olímpica del Poblenou, facing the beach and with direct access to wide pedestrian areas along the seafront. It is an ideal starting point for families, beginners and skaters of all ages and experience levels.

There is no deposit for inline skate rental. Wrist guards, knee pads and elbow pads are included, and a child helmet is free. Rental customers may also leave shoes, jackets, backpacks and small bags in the free luggage storage.

  • Flexible rental periods from one hour to multiple days
  • Protective gear options stated clearly before you rent
  • Direct links to current prices, availability and route guidance

How inline skate rental works

From checking availability to returning your skates

1. Choose a rental period

Check the published prices and ask on WhatsApp if you need a particular size, are coming with a group or want to rent on the same day.

2. Check the fit and protection

Your foot should feel secure without painful pressure. Check the fastenings, heel hold and the fit of wrist guards, knee pads and elbow pads before leaving.

3. Plan your return

Choose a route that suits your ability and return within the agreed opening hours. RSB opens daily from 10:30–13:30 and 16:30–20:00.

How long should you rent for?

Match the session to your experience, route and stops

1 hour

A good option for testing the skates, practising near the shop or taking a short trip around Port Olímpic.

2 or 3 hours

Enough time for part of the seafront at an easy pace, including breaks, without committing to a full day.

Full day or 24 hours

Better suited to experienced skaters who want a flexible route with more time for stops and sightseeing.

Where to skate from RSB

Three routes from the beachfront location in Vila Olímpica

Towards Hotel W

Head past Port Olímpic, Barceloneta and Sant Sebastià Beach towards Hotel W, following the seafront and adapting your speed at crossings and busy sections.

Towards Parc del Fòrum

Skate in the opposite direction via Nova Icària, Bogatell and Mar Bella towards Parc del Fòrum. This route passes the Mar Bella skateboard area and skatepark.

Parc de la Ciutadella and Zoo

For a greener urban alternative, head from Vila Olímpica towards Parc de la Ciutadella and the area beside Barcelona Zoo, following permitted paths and local signs.

Choose the route that suits your age, experience and rental time, and always give priority to pedestrians.

Fit, control and safety

A secure fit matters more than speed

Check the fit before you leave

Wear comfortable socks and make sure your heel does not lift inside the boot. The fastenings should hold your foot without pain or restricted circulation. Ask for another size if you feel severe pressure or excessive movement.

Practise the basics first

Test your balance, turns and braking before entering a busy area. Keep your knees slightly bent, leave enough stopping distance and adjust your speed around pedestrians, crossings and surface changes.

Read the conditions

Wet paving, sand, leaves and uneven surfaces reduce grip. Skate only where permitted, follow local signs and avoid headphones that block surrounding traffic and other users.
